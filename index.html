Summary
5 Key Findings
1
Zero-shot fails due to class-imbalance prior — not domain gap 94.8% non-hate EN training → strong non-hate prior. Recall = 0.080, Precision = 0.885. XLM-R-large is best at zero-shot — no negative transfer.
2
Few-shot transfer is stable and monotonically improving Hate F1: K=8 (0.590) → K=16 (0.619) → K=32 (0.649) → K=64 (0.694). No collapse. Class weights [1.0, 2.5] calibrated to Urdu's 40% hate rate.
3
Classical ML beats all few-shot neural methods TF-IDF + LR on full data: Hate F1 = 0.747. FuzzyWuzzy adds zero benefit — Latin lexicon vs Arabic Nastaliq script = complete mismatch.
4
Supervised XLM-R-large: strong performance, high FPR cost Hate F1 = 0.796 (+0.049 over TF-IDF+LR) but FPR rises 0.154 → 0.228. ~560× more compute for a 4.9-point gain — dominant signal is lexical.
5
20× Level-2 fairness disparity at zero-shot Interfaith recall = 28.2% vs Other recall = 1.4%. Annotator bias from Davidson 2017 AMT workers baked into EN weights — blind to native-Urdu-idiom hate.
